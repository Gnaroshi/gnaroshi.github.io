Reading method

Three-pass reading

  1. 1

    Pass 1 — Relevance

    Read the abstract and figures, then decide why the paper matters now.

  2. 2

    Pass 2 — Structure

    Follow the method, assumptions, experiments, and main limitations.

  3. 3

    Pass 3 — Details

    Work through equations, reproduce a result, or test part of the idea in code.
